# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is Moodle operators, training businesses, academies, and associations that want to sell learning without maintaining a separate ecommerce platform. Moodle developers and service providers are the secondary audience.

## Product Purpose

Modern Commerce turns Moodle into a complete learning storefront, connecting discovery, checkout, payment, enrolment, subscriptions, and customer operations in one system. The site must make the product tangible, earn technical trust, and move qualified visitors into a live demo.

## Positioning

Modern Commerce is the open-source commerce platform for Moodle: the complete path from offer to enrolled learner without a second website or fragile connector.

### Approved primary headline

> Every feature required to sell, fulfil, and operate a modern ecommerce business for training, eLearning courses, and digital products.

Use this statement as the primary website hero headline and the global footer positioning line. It broadens the commercial category beyond individual Moodle courses while retaining the complete operating-system promise. Supporting copy must still explain that ModernCommerce runs inside Moodle and should describe specific product types and fulfilment behaviour accurately.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: Explore the live demo.
- Secondary CTA: View on GitHub.
- Remembered line: Sell learning where learning happens.
- Belief ladder: purpose-built for Moodle; replaces a fragmented stack; supports real commerce workflows; inspectable and extensible; verifiable in a working demo.
- Proof: GPL-3.0-or-later licence, Moodle 5.2 compatibility, product source and documentation, four implemented gateways, storefront, checkout, subscriptions, operations, and learner workflows. Adoption and performance claims are not yet verified.

## Brand Personality

Assured, infrastructural, and generous. The experience should evoke confidence and momentum while making a technical product understandable.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic SaaS gradient template, corporate consulting brochure, card wall, or crypto landing page. Avoid unsupported “number one” badges, anonymous stock-photo heroes, excessive glass effects, and decorative code that proves nothing.

## Design Principles

1. Demonstrate the product before describing every feature.
2. Express leadership through specificity and confidence, not unverifiable superlatives.
3. Connect every commercial action to a learner outcome.
4. Make open source visible through licence, repository, documentation, and contribution paths.
5. Preserve the authority of Agunfon Interactivity LLC, USA while giving ModernCommerce a distinct product identity and a single product destination at moderncommerce.dev.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion support, semantic landmarks, meaningful alternatives, and accessible contrast.
