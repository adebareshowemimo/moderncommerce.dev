## What Advanced Pricing does

Advanced Pricing adds another pricing rule to an existing product. A **Sale** rule schedules promotional pricing for a date window. A **Tier** rule changes the amount for a defined quantity range. This tutorial creates a Tier rule.

## Open the product

From **Modern Commerce → Products**, locate the product you want to update and open its editor. In the Advanced Pricing section, select **Add Price** to open the pricing form.

## Choose the price type

Open **Price Type** and review both available options before selecting one:

- **Sale** applies promotional pricing within optional start and end dates.
- **Tier** applies pricing to a quantity range.

Select **Tier** for this example.

## Enter the pricing values

Enter the price customers pay in **Amount**. **Compare Amount** is optional and provides the higher reference amount used when presenting the offer.

Use **Minimum Quantity** and **Maximum Quantity** to define the range covered by the Tier rule. Leaving the maximum empty means the tier has no upper quantity boundary.

## Review dates and availability

**Start Date** and **End Date** are optional. Leaving both empty means the rule has no time window. The **Enabled** switch determines whether ModernCommerce can apply the rule; keep it enabled for an active rule.

## Save and verify

Select **Save Price**. Wait for the form to close, then confirm the Advanced Pricing table shows the selected type, amount, quantity range, date window and Active status. This persisted row confirms that the rule was saved.
