# Modern Commerce

---

**Modern Commerce** (`local_moderncommerce`) turns Moodle into a full ecommerce storefront. Sell courses, bundles, programs, and subscriptions from a branded, widget-driven storefront; take real payments through Stripe, PayPal, Paystack, or Flutterwave using your own merchant accounts; and auto-enrol buyers the moment payment clears — with invoices, coupons, enrolment keys, refunds, notifications, and analytics built in. Everything runs inside Moodle on cron, with no external service to host.
